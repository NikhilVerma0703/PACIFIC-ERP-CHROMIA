# Commercial module: questions, round four

Collected 2026-09-09 while building your round-three answers in. Your fifteen answers are recorded in DECISIONS-3.md with the rule each one set. These came out of building them: two places where the software now has a real gap, a few where your own files disagree with themselves, and some readings to confirm. The module runs on the default in brackets, so nothing waits on them.

## The two that leave a real gap
1. Cut-to-size and the dispatch check. A packing list of SLABS is checked slab by slab - bay 5 marks each one fit or unfit and nothing ships until the list is corrected (round one, answer 31). A cut-to-size list has no such column: a piece is not a slab and carries no fit or unfit. So today a cut-to-size list is passed the moment the checker opens it. Does bay 5 inspect cut pieces the same way, and if so is it piece by piece, or crate by crate? [default: a cut-to-size list needs no physical check and ships once packed - the slab rule is unchanged]
2. The duplicate barcode. Your file gives `220x19.5x2` and `220x15x2` the SAME code `8720847172266`, and `126x25x2` arrived as `'8720847172297` with a stray apostrophe. I strip the apostrophe. I cannot tell which of the two sizes owns that code, so both are saved with a warning rather than one being silently picked. Which is right, or is a third code missing? [default: both stay as sent, flagged on the screen, and nothing is guessed]

## The labels
3. How big should the barcode print? I set it at one and a half times the EAN reference size, on the reasoning that this is read off a crate in a yard with a hand scanner at arm's length, not at a shop till. The standard allows anywhere from 0.8 to 2.0 times. If your scanner or your customer's has a preference, it is one number. [default: 1.5x, which is 56 mm of bars on a 100 x 70 mm label]
4. Where does SHIPPING DATE come from? Your crate labels all read 07-09-2026. Is that the container's sailing date, the date the list was finalised, or a date typed per shipment? [default: typed on the packing list, falling back to the day it was finalised]
5. The item code. Your file prints `CQBE 101x19.5x2` - the code and the size together. Do you want to store just `CQBE` and let the ERP add the size (so a size change cannot leave a stale code), or store the whole string exactly as the customer sent it? The label prints the same either way. [default: store what you type, and the ERP prints the size once whichever you chose]
6. Piece labels for cut-to-size. Your DS-Thresholds sheet is one line, `CQBE 103X11X2`, repeated per piece. A cut-to-size piece also has a piece number (1A, 2B) and a room (KITCHEN, BATH ROOM VANITY). Should those print on the piece label too, so a fitter on site knows where the piece goes? [default: item code and size only, exactly like your thresholds sheet]

## The cut-to-size sheet
7. Your three workbooks carry a header block the module has nowhere to put: CONSIGNMENT, PACIFIC COLOUR NAME (e.g. CPKT12412A) and PROJECT # (VILLAGE-43-4). We hold the PO and the crate count. Should the packing list gain a consignment reference and a project number? [default: not added; the PO number and the design code carry what we have]
8. Weight. Your sheets give a weight per line and a total per crate. Is that the stone alone, or the stone plus the crate? The invoice and the export documents need to know which. [default: the stone alone, with the crate's own weight added on the packing list where it already exists]
9. Sizes. Those sheets head the same numbers "SIZE(Inches)" on one block and "SIZE(IN CM)" on another, and the numbers are millimetres in both. I store millimetres and print in the unit the packing list is set to. Confirm that is right. [default: stored in millimetres, printed in cm or inches as the list says]
