# Shift Production Incentive

**Pacific Surfaces — Production (Silos → Mixer → Distributor/Kreos → Robo → Press → Oven → Jot)**

Effective from: ______________  ·  Reviewed monthly

---

## What this is

Every month, each shift earns a **score**. The shift with the highest score earns the
highest incentive. The incentive is paid as a **percentage of your own salary** — so
everyone on the shift shares the same result, but each person's amount is based on their
own pay.

**Production is a team game.** One person cannot win this alone, and one person cannot
lose it alone. The silo team, the mixer, the distributor, the press, the oven and Jot all
count as one shift. You win together.

---

## How the score is calculated

Your score is built from **two things only** — how much you made, and how good it was.

```
        SCORE  =  QUANTITY  ×  QUALITY
```

They are **multiplied**, not added. This matters:

- 1,000 slabs made badly → low score
- 100 slabs made perfectly → low score
- **Many slabs made well → high score**

You cannot make up bad quality by producing more, and you cannot make up low production
by being careful with a few slabs. You need both.

### 1. QUANTITY — how many slabs

The number of slabs your shift produced that reached **Jot** and were measured.

### 2. QUALITY — what QC grades those same slabs

Quality follows **your** slabs. We take the slabs your shift pressed and look at the grade
QC finally gave them — not whatever was being polished during your hours, which is
someone else's work.

| QC grade | Counts as |
|---|---|
| **A** (and A2) | **100%** |
| B | 50% |
| C (reject) | **0%** |

Your grade share is then scored against a **90% minimum standard** — you are paid for how
far **above** 90% you get:

| Your grade share | Quality score |
|---|---|
| 90% or below | **0%** |
| 95% | 50% |
| 98% | 80% |
| 100% | **100%** |

A slab still waiting to be polished is **not** counted against you — it simply waits until
QC grades it.

## ⚠️ IMPORTANT — Log in to your OWN shift only

**The score is counted from the MIS entry. If you are logged in during another shift, your
work is counted in THEIR score — not yours.**

This is the single most common way a shift loses points it had already earned.

### Rules

1. **Log in at the start of your shift.** No login = no entry = no score for that hour.
2. **Enter every hour in MIS.** Missing hours are missing production. The system cannot
   count what was never entered.
3. **LOG OUT at the end of your shift.** If you stay logged in, the next shift's slabs are
   recorded under your name and hours — and your own next shift may show nothing.
4. **Never enter data for another shift.** If an hour was missed, tell your incharge — do
   not fill it in under the wrong shift.
5. **Enter the correct shift** (1 / 2 / 3) on every record. If the shift is wrong, the
   slabs go to the wrong team.

> **We check this.** The system compares the shift written on each record against the
> actual clock time. Records logged under the wrong shift are visible in the report and
> will be corrected — which may move points from one shift to another.

---

## What you must record for the score to count

The score can only count what is recorded against your shift.

- Enter the **slab class** (3 cm / 2 cm / 1.2 cm) on every Jot record.
- Enter every **MIS hour** for your shift, with the correct **shift number** and the
  **incharge names**.

If a shift measures only a few slabs, its quality score is built from those few slabs
only. **Measure everything — it is your own score.**

---

## How the money is decided

1. At the end of the month, every shift's score is totalled.
2. Shifts are **ranked** by score.
3. Each rank carries an incentive **percentage of salary**.
4. Everyone on that shift receives that percentage **of their own salary**.

| Rank | Incentive |
|---|---|
| 🥇 1st | ______ % of salary |
| 🥈 2nd | ______ % of salary |
| 🥉 3rd | ______ % of salary |

**Conditions**

- A shift must meet the **minimum quality standard** to take first place — the highest
  quantity alone does not win.
- **Safety comes first.** Any lost-time accident in the shift means no incentive for that
  month, regardless of score.
- Scores and rankings are **published every month** and can be checked. If you believe a
  number is wrong, raise it with your incharge — every point can be traced back to the
  individual slab records behind it.
