Pacific Italian Surfaces exhibits at the 60th Marmomac, Veronafiere, Verona, 22-25 September 2026.

The stall code appears as "Stall No- 10 F 2" in Pacific - Logo Placement.pdf, and the booth
render prints only "STALL NO. F2". I read that as **Hall 10, Stand F2** and used it in the
invite video, but the hall number is an inference, not a confirmed fact - check it against the
Marmomac exhibitor listing before anything else goes public.

Samples shipped: 29 granite pieces (517.86 sq ft) and 30 quartz pieces (1051.67 sq ft),
totalling 1,569 sq ft. Granite finishes are named Eco Antique, Mountain Minnow, Super Rock,
Gator, Lithium Plus, Brisket, Linea, Catena, Bamboo, River. Eclipse is the large-format
printed quartz line (Almond Mist, Patagonia, Taj Vein, Statuario, Arlina).

Every Marmomac stand render in "Marmomac p[lcement" carries red annotation arrows and
non-brand type, so it is unusable as footage; the real booth photos in Application Images
folder 17 are clean. Related: [[pacific-marmomac-invite-video]].
