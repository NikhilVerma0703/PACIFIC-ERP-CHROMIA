Pacific Italian Surfaces display bay at AMA Ceramics, Sarjapur Rd, Bengaluru.
Rev A issued 2026-09-15; owner presentation 2026-09-17. Project lives in
`C:\Users\user\Downloads\AMA-Pacific-Sarjapur\`.

Bay is **4775 × 5207 × 3353 mm** (15′-8″ × 17′-1″ × 11′-0″) and is **open at both
short ends** — there is no back wall to run services in, so all LED drivers sit in
the counter plinth.

**The street wall is not solid.** It is a slab screen standing in front of the
shopfront glazing, with a circular porthole cut through it. The brief reads as
though the whole wall could be clad; doing that would kill the bay's only daylight
and its only visibility from the road. Scheme keeps it, rebuilt at Ø1600 with a lit
bronze collar. This is the one point we pushed back on.

The existing fit-out carries **Paradigm** branding — a competitor — and is stripped
out in full.

Everything (drawings, the Blender model *and* the browser walkthrough) is
generated from `_scripts/project.py`, so changing a dimension there and
re-running regenerates the whole set consistently. See [[blender-5-action-api]]
and [[webgl-file-protocol-textures]] for the two gotchas hit while building it.

Three published artifacts, all private until shared:
presentation `Wm8rSrtY7mtiAgaRJBzavk`, navigable 3D walkthrough
`3RcsYnvzLmRwTcJRcc5Kvk`, material/layout configurator `UfqP6VJGzLXMVNkzEXC3uq`.
The walkthrough deep-links: `?start=1&view=2&scene=night`.

Five decisions still block production: porthole rebuild vs blank; onyx tunable
2700–4000 K vs fixed; who picks the B1 statement block; working kitchen vs display
only; and a site re-measure (the survey is approximate, taken off photographs).
